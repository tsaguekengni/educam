Unit 3 · Week 2 · 45 minutes · downloaded for offline use

1. Discovery activity

15 min

Ayuk comes back from Buea market with a loaf of bread. His sister Bih is waiting. “Shall we share it?” asks Ayuk. But careful: each one must get exactly the same share, otherwise it is not fair.

When we share one whole thing into two equal parts, each part is called a half. We write it 1/2. If we share it into four equal parts, each part is a quarter, and we write 1/4.

Summary — copy into the exercise book

A fraction is a share in equal parts.
1/2 is read “a half”. 1/4 is read “a quarter”.
Two quarters make a half: 1/4 + 1/4 = 1/2.
